Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Plain-language summary: this website doesn't use cookies, analytics, or trackers. The contact and booking forms don't submit to a server — they open a pre-filled email in your own email app, addressed to us. We only see what you choose to send.

Who we are

This site is operated by Springbok Travels & Tours, based in Victoria Falls, Zimbabwe. For any privacy question, contact us at springboktravelsandtours@gmail.com or +263 773 410 547.

Information we collect

We only receive information you actively choose to send us — for example, through the contact form, the booking form, WhatsApp, phone, or email. This may include your name, email address, travel dates, number of guests, and anything else you include in a message.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking scripts, and does not automatically collect browsing data.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or booking request
  • To plan and confirm your itinerary
  • To communicate with you before, during and after your trip

We don't use your information for anything beyond arranging and delivering the trip you've enquired about, unless you separately agree to something else (for example, being added to a mailing list).

How it's shared

We don't sell or rent your information. We may share the minimum necessary details with third parties directly involved in delivering your trip — for example, a lodge, park authority, or transport partner — solely to fulfil your booking.

How it's stored

Messages sent through this site's forms go straight to our email inbox via your own email client; they aren't stored in a database on this website. Information you send us by email or WhatsApp is retained only as long as needed to plan your trip and handle any after-trip questions.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details above and we'll action this promptly.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle information changes — for instance, if we add analytics or online payments in future — we'll update this page and revise the "last updated" date above.
