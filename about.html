About Springbok

Local guides. Global standards. One team you can reach on WhatsApp at any hour.

We started Springbok Travels & Tours because the best way to see Zimbabwe isn't through a fixed brochure itinerary — it's through people who actually live here.

We're based in Victoria Falls, and every guide on our team works and travels across the routes we sell — the trail into the rainforest below the Falls, the Big 5 circuits of Hwange, the walking paths of Mana Pools. That means the advice you get before you book, and the plan we build once you do, comes from firsthand knowledge rather than a script.

We keep group sizes small on purpose. A safari vehicle with four seats sees more, moves quieter, and lets your guide actually talk you through what you're looking at — not just drive you past it. The same thinking applies to how we plan: tell us your dates, your interests and your budget, and we'll shape the trip around that, rather than fitting you into a package that already exists.

We also work with conservation and community partners across the parks we operate in, because a safari industry that doesn't put something back doesn't have much of a future.
